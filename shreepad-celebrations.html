About Shreepad Celebrations

Shreepad Celebrations is an ultra-premium completed residential project by the renowned Shreepad Group, located on Gaurav Path Road in Palanpur, Surat. The project offers exclusive 3 BHK apartments with 19-foot wide spacious DECK balconies and a massive 28,000 sq.ft landscape area.

Spread across approximately 1.98 acres with 4 towers and 208 units, Shreepad Celebrations features 10-foot floor heights, rooftop solar panels, and follows green building concepts. The project is RERA registered (PR/GJ/SURAT/SURAT CITY/SUDA/RAA01930/A1R/261218) and offers ready possession since 2021.

Celebrations begins with family — every detail has been designed to make everyday life a celebration, from the premium interiors to the lush green surroundings. The location provides excellent connectivity to Rajhans Cinema, Bhulka Vihar School, and major hospitals.
