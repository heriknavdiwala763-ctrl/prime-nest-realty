About Nakshatra Solitaire

Nakshatra Solitaire is an iconic residential landmark developed by the prestigious Happy Home Group, strategically located on Palanpur Canal Road in Palanpur, Surat. Built to deliver a premium community living experience, the project features thoughtfully designed 2, 3, and 4 BHK luxury residences.

With 288 exclusive families residing across 13 beautifully elevated towers, Nakshatra Solitaire boasts an extensive array of world-class amenities including a sparkling swimming pool, fully equipped AC fitness center, grand clubhouse, and landscaped gardens with senior citizen sit-outs.

The project offers unmatched connectivity to Gaurav Path, Palanpur Jakat Naka, reputed schools, shopping avenues, and top-tier hospitals, ensuring a life of ultimate comfort and prestige for your entire family.
