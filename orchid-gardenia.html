About Orchid Gardenia

Orchid Gardenia by Orchid Infra & Kiyan Group is an expansive 3-acre gated residential community located prime on Gauravpath Road in Palanpur, Surat. Comprising 8 magnificent high-rise towers with 416 thoughtfully planned homes, Orchid Gardenia brings comfort, security, and convenience together.

Each apartment is vastu-compliant and engineered for maximum sunlight, cross-ventilation, and spacious balcony spaces. The township features a lavish entrance foyer, lush landscaped lawns, 2 high-speed elevators per tower, and 24/7 power backup.
